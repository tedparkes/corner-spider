# Future ideas

These are future directions, not features included in this release or promised dates.

- **Custom skins:** color pickers for named regions such as abdomen, front body,
  legs, markings, mouthparts and palps. Preview before applying; save named local
  presets. SkinCatalog already separates these palette regions.
- **More food:** beyond the existing flies and Portia spider prey, crickets, mealworms and other appropriate jumper prey, with
  distinct locomotion, stalking and capture animations. Preserve the occasional,
  uncluttered desktop experience and one-prey limit.
- **Other species:** additional jumping spiders and eventually other spider types,
  with suitable proportions, leg rigs and behavior rather than palette swaps alone.
- **An optional background with depth:** a living scene with perches and surfaces
  the spider can walk around, occlusion and spatial movement. Keep the transparent
  desktop pet available, support multiple monitors, preserve normal clicks and
  provide a clear performance budget.
- **Release polish:** code signing, broader hands-on mixed-DPI and suspend/resume
  coverage, accessible keyboard review of settings and longer resource soaks.
