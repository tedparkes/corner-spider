CORNER SPIDER 1.0 RC4 (1.0.0-rc.4)

NEW: Portia's prey web is about three times wider and taller, with a larger
resident in the center. Portia's patrols avoid this corner until its deliberate
approach and edge-plucking sequence. Silk remains until Portia leaves after the
meal, then fades. Small work areas fit the web to leave room for the approach.

Settings > Appearance shows the selected species' web in the upper-right
corner of the skin preview. Skin, size and background changes update it live.

The sleeping retreat has a connected support mesh and stronger attachments
to both desktop edges. Silk appearance varies with species, and growth keeps
existing threads in place. The small prey spider builds a less regular, better
anchored capture web. Saved nest progress and end-entry behavior are preserved.
See docs\SILK-REFERENCES.md for evidence and artistic choices.

Also included: Portia corner-web encounters, species personalities, and an optional
"Buy me a coffee / Monster" banner using the original Cash App QR. Click the QR
to enlarge it; the recipient is $tedparkes. No payment is needed to use the pet.

Select Portia, then Settings > Behavior > Invite a web-building spider. It builds
for 3-4 minutes, waits 1-2 minutes, then Portia approaches, plucks and ambushes.
Keep it running; the full encounter takes several minutes. Only one prey exists.
The tray feeding action remains a quicker ordinary hunt.

Appearance explains each species personality. Behavior can disable personalities
or web encounters. Research and artistic choices: docs\BEHAVIOR-RESEARCH.md.

All twenty skins now pounce from about 3.5 times farther away when space
allows. They still stalk, line up, reach and crouch, then make a quick leap.
The distance scales with pet size and display DPI; already-close prey gets a
shorter jump. Both flies and Portia's spider prey use the longer hunting leap.

Portia can hunt small crawling spiders as well as flies. Select Portia
in Appearance, then use "Release a small spider" from the tray to try it.
Automatic prey is a mix of flies and small spiders for Portia. The existing
prey toggle and interval control both, with only one prey present at a time.

Twenty skins with species-shaped bodies, different leg proportions and
articulated palps that clean the eyes and chelicerae. The original nine species
have male/female pairs, plus Portia labiata and a stylized green Hyllus diardi
female. Appearance preview demonstrates the new grooming animation.

The spider walks around the nest, lines up at an end opening and crawls
inside head first. It unfolds and leaves through the other end on waking.
Try "Go to hammock" and "Wake up" from the tray menu.

Click during a finger ride to have the spider choose a spot above, turn,
brace and jump upward with silk. Near the top edge, it makes a safe side hop.

Settings > Finger has a skin-tone slider from deep brown (0%) to very pale
(100%), with a live preview. The chosen shade is saved between sessions.

A layered silk retreat is anchored to both corner edges. It keeps its cocoon
shape, with room to approach its ends. Existing nest progress is kept.
A jumping spider for your actual Windows desktop.

START
Extract the entire folder. Double-click JumpingSpider.exe or Start-Spider.cmd.
Requires Windows 10/11 and .NET Framework 4.8. No administrator rights or downloads.
The source ZIP needs Build.cmd first; the Windows ZIP is already compiled.

SETTINGS
Right-click the spider tray icon and select Settings, or double-click the icon.
Appearance: twenty skins, live preview, dark/light background, rotation, 20-180% size.
Finger: skin-tone slider from deep brown to very pale, with a light/dark preview.
Behavior sliders: prey interval, time before finger appears, approach distance,
loss-of-interest distance, and fast-movement escape threshold.
Desktop: corner, hammock screen, all monitors, opt-in Windows startup.
The rightmost screen is the default home. Startup is off by default.
Preferences apply live and save automatically. Reset keeps nest progress and meals.

PLAY
Hold beside an awake spider. The finger starts fading in after 4.5 seconds by
default. The spider can jump on, hang, climb, explore and wave. Carry gently.
Click for a planned upward hop; fast movement startles it. Clicks still reach
your apps. The offered finger holds still briefly until takeoff, while the real
pointer returns immediately. A hanging spider climbs onto the finger first.
Leave room around the pointer for the finger and spider. The real pointer returns
when play ends, on pause, or on exit; a short-lived helper handles crash recovery.
Approach and exit distances are adjustable and do not shrink with the pet's size.

QUICK ACTIONS
The tray keeps feeding (a small spider for Portia, a fly for other skins), Go to hammock, Wake up, Pause/Resume, Settings and Quit.
Only one prey animal is present. Most of the spider's day is spent resting near corners.
Silk and preferences persist in LocalAppData\CornerSpider, with a previous-save backup.

EXIT / REMOVE
Quit from the tray or run Stop-Spider.cmd. Disable startup before moving or removing
the app; after moving, re-enable startup from the new copy if desired.
Delete the extracted folder to remove; optionally delete the local save folder.
