# RC10 skin review

All 20 options were reviewed against species descriptions and inspected on light
and dark backgrounds. An independent critic reviewed the first pass, then checked
the revised galleries. The principal issue was a shared dense fur outline that
made narrow-legged species look like recolored, robust Phidippus.

The renderer now separates hair density, hair length and foreleg thickness from
joint geometry. Portia retains exposed thin shafts between localized brushes,
an irregular narrow abdomen, a high angular front body and larger middle lateral
eyes. The flat bark/wall species use matte shading and shorter setae. Joint
lengths, foot contact, grooming targets and existing movement bounds are retained.
Forward-facing eyes, small highlights and expressive poses carry the cute style.

| Option | Review and resulting treatment |
| --- | --- |
| Regal female | Retain warm coloring and fuller abdomen; add localized dorsal tufts. |
| Regal male | Retain black/ivory, basal band, foreleg fringes and larger palp bulbs. |
| Bold female | Retain full shape; narrow the two rear pale marks. |
| Bold male | Retain dark robust coat, smaller abdomen and green mouthparts; narrow rear marks. |
| Zebra female | Shorten setae and reduce foreleg bulk; split the pale transverse bands slightly. |
| Zebra male | Same short coat; retain smaller abdomen and make the projecting chelicerae darker and tapered. |
| Tan female | Lengthen the low silhouette, use matte shading and shorter leg setae; retain dark exposed mouthparts. |
| Tan male | Same flat treatment with smaller abdomen; add pale mouth-covering hairs. |
| Red-backed female | Concentrate the dark abdominal stripe in the posterior half; darken head/leg hairs separately from the red abdomen. |
| Red-backed male | Retain solid red abdomen and robust dark front body; separate dark head/leg fur from abdominal color. |
| Emerald female | Four pairs of pale spots, third pair elongated, subtle orange side marks, brown legs and darker foreleg bands; shorter coat. |
| Emerald male | Same marking corrections with darker dorsal color and smaller abdomen. |
| Gray wall female | Longer matte body, shorter setae and orange clypeal band. |
| Gray wall male | Darker head, central and lateral pale head stripes, pale clypeal connection and small cheliceral V. |
| Pantropical female | Retain robust brown body, dark eye field and subdued rear markings; reduce uniform fuzz. |
| Pantropical male | Retain bold central stripe and rear spots; shorten setae without flattening the front body. |
| Apache female | Retain orange-red front/body sections; break up the median abdominal stripe edge. |
| Apache male | Retain orange-red color and green mouthparts; correct the palps to dark brown/black. |
| Portia labiata female | Fine exposed legs with discrete brushes, narrow irregular abdomen, angular front body, pale facial crescent and larger middle lateral eyes. |
| Hyllus diardi green female | Retain broad head, long full abdomen and eye-region tufts; shorten head setae. Green remains a requested fantasy palette. |

Skins are stylized adult illustrations informed by species references, not
identification models. Proportions and markings are simplified for desktop
readability. Natural variation, age, viewing angle and lighting can change the
appearance of real spiders. The green Hyllus is an intentional fantasy color
variant. At 20% size, fine markings become tiny; the larger preview shows detail.

The second critique confirmed the improved Portia silhouette and caught a missing
Gray wall male head stripe, which was restored. A suspected combined-gallery
rendering defect was checked against original PNG pixels and withdrawn: the saved
artwork was complete. No rendering change was made on that unsupported finding.

## References used by the critic

- [Wanless, revision of Portia, pp. 84 and 103–105](https://peckhamia.com/library/Wanless%201978%20A%20revision%20of%20the%20spider%20genus%20Portia.pdf): angular carapace, brushes, female facial scales and eye proportions.
- [UF/IFAS, Regal jumping spider](https://ask.ifas.ufl.edu/publication/IN309): Regal sex differences and comparison with Bold.
- [Penn State, Bold jumper](https://extension.psu.edu/bold-jumper-spider): robust coat, marks and mouthparts.
- [British Arachnological Society, Salticus](https://britishspiders.org.uk/sites/default/files/2020-08/Salticus_spreads.pdf) and [Penn State, Zebra jumper](https://extension.psu.edu/zebra-jumper): transverse bands and male chelicerae.
- [Missouri Department of Conservation, Tan jumping spider](https://mdc.mo.gov/discover-nature/field-guide/tan-jumping-spider): flattened form and facial sex differences.
- [USDA IDtools, Phidippus johnsoni](https://idtools.org/tools/25/index.cfm?entityID=916&packageID=91): red abdomen and female stripe.
- [Penn State, Emerald jumper](https://extension.psu.edu/emerald-jumper): four pale spot pairs, orange spots and leg colors.
- [UF/IFAS, Gray wall and Pantropical jumpers](https://ask.ifas.ufl.edu/publication/IN315): head stripes, face color and sex differences.
- [Animal Diversity Web, Phidippus apacheanus](https://animaldiversity.org/accounts/Phidippus_apacheanus/) and [Edwards revision](https://thefsca.org/wp-content/uploads/2025/01/occasional-papers-vol-11.pdf): Apache color and palp differences.
- [Hoang, 2025 redescription, pp. 284–285](https://europeanjournaloftaxonomy.eu/index.php/ejt/article/download/2831/12915/): Hyllus female proportions and natural coloration.

All artwork is procedural. No reference photographs were copied into the app.
