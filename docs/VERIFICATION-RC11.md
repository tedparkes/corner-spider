cursor-restoration and focus suite last passed on RC10; that evidence is preserved
in [RC10 verification](VERIFICATION-RC10.md) and [Windows diagnostics](windows-checks.txt).

display layouts; physical mixed-DPI changes, unplug/reconnect and sleep/resume
remain manual checks. A long RC11 resource soak has not been performed.

wait, confirm she settles near a side's anchored end, turns toward the resident,
and keeps watching it through five plucks and the attack. Repeat in another corner
