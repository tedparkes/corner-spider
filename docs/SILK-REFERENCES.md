# Silk and retreat artwork — 1.0 RC2

The sleeping retreat now rests on a connected silk support mesh, with attachment
bundles spread along both adjacent desktop edges. Fine cross ties end on actual
support threads. Contact patches follow the edges, and the bundles merge into
the sleeve. This visual support is present even in a young nest; saved progress
adds silk without rearranging established threads.

The corner, translucency, attachment spacing and animation proportions are desktop
adaptations. A monitor edge stands in for a surface such as bark or a leaf. Most
of these jumpers build silk retreats rather than a classic radial capture web.
The same diagonal axis and two entrances remain usable. No photographs or external
assets are required; silk is drawn procedurally and retained between frames.

The other male/female pairs share their species' silk profile. Age, purpose,
reproductive state and available attachment surfaces can matter as much as species.
The app's variations should not be used to identify a real spider.

The little web resident remains a **generic prey spider**, not a named species.
Its capture web uses a recognizable orb-style illustration: five desktop-edge
attachments, an uneven outer frame, sixteen radial spokes and a continuous curved
capture spiral winding toward the hub. Slightly varied spacing and inward sag
avoid the former crossed-line network. This is an artistic choice for the generic
resident, not a claim about a specific prey species. The same curved geometry
controls silk construction and the builder's movement. The 3-4 minute build and
1-2 minute wait remain app design choices; this is separate from Portia's own
sleeping platform.

For readability, the resident's web is now three times its earlier linear span
(564 logical pixels across at 100% pet size), fitted down only when the selected
work area needs room for Portia's approach. This is a display choice, not a
biological size claim. The resident waits at the central junction; Portia plucks
an outer anchor and draws the resident along their connecting strand. Ordinary
patrols reserve this corner, and spent silk remains until the hunter moves clear.
